    How we make a commercial

    1. Script
    2. Storyboard

    It starts as a script.

    Before we shoot anything, we write it. This is a real page from our ZestBot TV commercial — a kirana shop owner losing customers to online apps, until a superhero lands on his supply truck.

    Highlighted: the problem, the hero and the promise — what every ad needs.

    Then we draw every shot.

    Camera angle, action and dialogue are sketched frame by frame, so the shoot day runs fast and on budget. These are the original ZestBot storyboard panels.

    We shoot it with our own crew.
