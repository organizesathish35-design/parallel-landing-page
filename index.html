            How much does video production cost in Bangalore?

            It depends on the script: locations, cast, shoot days and animation. Tell us your budget range and we'll shape the idea to fit it, or tell you honestly if it won't.

            We don't have a script yet. Can you write one?

            Yes. Bring a brief, a product or just a launch date. Our writers come up with the concept, script and dialogue, and you sign off before anything is shot.

            Which languages do you make ads in?

            English, Kannada, Telugu and Hindi. We write and record each version, so it sounds native, not dubbed.

            Do we get versions for YouTube, Instagram and OTT?

            Yes. Along with the TV master, we deliver the cuts and formats you need for OTT and social.

            Do you make UGC video ads for Meta and YouTube?

            Yes. We script them, cast creators who fit your buyers and shoot them to feel native on Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, including Shorts. You get several hooks and cutdowns to test.

            How long does it take?

            It depends on the shoot. Share your on-air date and we'll plan the script, storyboard, shoot and edit backwards from it.

            Can you make an ad without a shoot?

            Yes. For product demos, explainers or tight timelines, we make the ad in 2D, 3D or AI animation, still written, directed and sound-designed by our team.
